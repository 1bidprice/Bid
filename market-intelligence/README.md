# Investor Control — Market Intelligence Engine

Status: foundation / not yet production

## Purpose

This subsystem turns verified market evidence into structured, reviewable investment signals. It is not a generic chatbot and it never executes trades.

The production flow is:

`sources -> identity resolution -> evidence records -> contradiction checks -> deterministic metrics -> AI synthesis -> ranked signal -> user review -> outcome tracking`

## Hard rules

1. No signal without a timestamp and source trail.
2. Facts, calculations and model estimates are stored separately.
3. One primary source is sufficient for a factual event. Otherwise at least two independent reliable sources are required.
4. Social media cannot be the only evidence for a recommendation.
5. Critical numbers are calculated by code, not generated by an LLM.
6. An LLM may summarise evidence and compare scenarios, but may not invent missing metrics.
7. Every signal has an invalidation condition and review date.
8. Signals are versioned. Old recommendations are never silently rewritten.
9. Backtests must prevent look-ahead bias and include fees, spread, liquidity and FX where relevant.
10. No automatic order execution.

## Source hierarchy

1. Regulatory filings and exchange disclosures.
2. Official issuer investor-relations material.
3. Licensed/reputable financial news.
4. Market and fundamental datasets.
5. Alternative data as supporting evidence only.

## Initial coverage

Phase A prioritises quality over headline coverage:

- current portfolio and watchlist,
- Athens Exchange,
- S&P 500,
- Nasdaq-100.

The architecture supports later expansion to all active NYSE/Nasdaq and Euronext regulated equities, followed by other exchanges only where data rights and quality are adequate.

## Signal categories

- `QUALITY_COMPOUNDER`
- `VALUE_REPRICING`
- `EVENT_DRIVEN`
- `SPECULATIVE_CATALYST`
- `MOMENTUM_CONFIRMED`
- `TURNAROUND`
- `INCOME_STABILITY`
- `DETERIORATION`
- `EVENT_RISK`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

## Required output

A market signal must include:

- canonical company identity,
- category and suggested action,
- reference price and time,
- time horizon,
- evidence list,
- facts,
- calculations,
- thesis,
- bullish and bearish scenarios,
- catalysts,
- risks,
- invalidation condition,
- confidence and data-quality scores,
- review date,
- version and status.

A bare `BUY` or `SELL` is invalid output.

## First implementation milestone

- JSON schemas for company identity, evidence and market signals.
- deterministic evidence gate and ranking function,
- tests that block unsupported recommendations,
- mock signals for Allwyn and Virgin Galactic,
- read-only mobile signal screen,
- then real ingestion and AI synthesis.

## Training strategy

The first system is not fine-tuned on live news. Fresh information belongs in retrieval and evidence storage, not in model weights.

Learning comes from a versioned outcome dataset:

- 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 3 month, 6 month and 12 month returns,
- maximum drawdown,
- catalyst confirmed or rejected,
- confidence calibration,
- performance by category, market, sector and event type.

Only after a sufficiently large, reviewed dataset exists should fine-tuning be considered.
