# MINBEIS 5-Minute Funding Demo Script

Purpose: demonstrate a real product and technical moat without making unsupported investment-performance claims.

## 0:00–0:45 — User problem

Show the portfolio summary.

Explain:
- position value, cost and P/L are calculated from canonical portfolio accounting;
- incomplete or unverified market data fails closed instead of showing invented values;
- holdings remain installation-owned and are not silently transferred to another user.

## 0:45–1:30 — Portfolio intelligence

Show:
- total portfolio value;
- allocation/concentration;
- P/L contribution;
- portfolio history ranges.

State clearly that local portfolio history begins when verified observations are collected; no fake pre-install portfolio history is generated.

## 1:30–2:20 — Decision discipline

Open MINBEIS decision controls / opportunity layer.

Show:
- evidence/research inputs;
- confidence and data quality;
- blockers;
- human approval requirement;
- no automatic broker execution.

Position the product as decision support with auditability, not an autonomous trading bot.

## 2:20–3:20 — Learning system

Show or explain from the technical dashboard/evidence:
- immutable Decision Journal;
- context hash;
- 7/30/90-day outcomes;
- max favourable/adverse excursion;
- review queue;
- context learning by market regime/event/reason.

Key sentence:
"MINBEIS does not call a past decision a success or failure and rewrite itself immediately. It collects the later outcome and sends potential lessons through controlled validation."

## 3:20–4:15 — Historical replay without hindsight

Show technical evidence for:
- event availability timestamp;
- evidence published after replay `asOf` excluded;
- undated evidence blocked;
- future candles excluded from decision input;
- future outcomes evaluation-only.

Key sentence:
"The system is designed so historical testing cannot see tomorrow's news."

## 4:15–4:50 — Prospective challenger

Show:
- learning proposal;
- frozen evidence hash;
- proposal creation timestamp;
- only later decisions entering shadow validation;
- promotion gate and chronological stability requirements;
- automatic promotion disabled.

Key sentence:
"A better-looking rule is not promoted because it wins a backtest. It must survive new data after it was proposed."

## 4:50–5:00 — Funding ask transition

Close with:
"Funding is intended to turn this validated architecture into a broadly tested product: licensed data, external beta, regulatory/legal validation, production distribution and enough prospective observations to measure whether the learning system adds real value."

Do not end the demo with a return promise or investment recommendation.
