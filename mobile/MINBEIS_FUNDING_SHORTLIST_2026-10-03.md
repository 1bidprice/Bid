## 1. NBG Business Seeds — 17th Innovation & Technology Competition

Official source:
https://www.nbg.gr/en/business/business-seeds/news-events/news-nbg-business-seeds-17th-competition-submission-period-2026

Submission deadline:
14 October 2026, 15:00.

Relevant themes:
- Fintech
- Digital Business & Ecosystems
- Advanced Technologies & AI
- Financial Empowerment

Published support:
- first three proposals: €20,000 / €12,000 / €8,000;
- additional special awards;
- total monetary prizes stated by NBG: €60,000;
- mentoring, networking and publicity;
- NBG states that shortlisted proposals may also be explored for partnership, services and possible equity participation.

Strong thematic fit because the product combines fintech, decision intelligence, AI governance and financial empowerment.

Prepare a competition-specific submission using the funding one-pager and governed-learning evidence. Do not claim proven alpha or autonomous self-improvement.

## 2. Elevate Greece — National Startup Registry

Official source:
https://elevategreece.gov.gr/el/mitroo/

Current 3rd annual application cycle:
1 September – 30 December.

Key documented conditions include:
- eligible Greek legal entity types include IKE, EPE and AE;
- Greek entity / Greek tax identity requirements apply;
- startup age and SME thresholds apply;
- evaluation includes innovation and scalability;
- the standardized product must be scalable rather than bespoke consulting.

Benefits described by Elevate Greece include:
- international visibility;
- networking;
- ecosystem/support measures;
- exposure to VC funds and angel investors.

Important:
Registry membership does not automatically guarantee state aid or VC funding.

MINBEIS fit:
Potentially strong after legal-entity eligibility is confirmed. The governed-learning architecture strengthens the innovation/scalability case.

Immediate action:
Confirm legal-entity eligibility before preparing an application.

## 4. EIC Accelerator 2026

Official source:
https://eic.ec.europa.eu/funding-opportunities_en

Call status:
Open.

2026 full-proposal batching dates include:
7 Jan, 4 Mar, 6 May, 8 Jul, 2 Sep, 4 Nov 2026.

Official FAQ:
https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-frequently-asked-questions/faqs-eic-accelerator_en

The EIC FAQ states short proposals are evaluated monthly and results can take 4–6 weeks; it recommends submitting a short proposal around 10 weeks before the intended full-proposal batch.

MINBEIS fit:
Not treated as an immediate 4 November target unless the project already has a successful short-proposal GO. Better positioned as a later-stage route after external validation, commercial evidence and a stronger high-risk/high-impact innovation case.

Immediate action:
Build toward a future EIC-quality package rather than rush an unsupported application.

## Claim discipline for every application

Use:
- CI-verified product capabilities;
- audited decision journal architecture;
- strict point-in-time replay;
- prospective shadow validation;
- human-controlled promotion gate;
- transparent open gaps.

Do not use:
- guaranteed return language;
- "market prediction" claims;
- unproven alpha claims;
- statements that the model has already learned enough to outperform benchmarks;
- claims of production signing until a non-debug release certificate is in place.
