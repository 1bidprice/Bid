# MINBEIS — Funding One-Pager

## Product

MINBEIS is an auditable investment decision-support system for retail investors. It combines portfolio accounting, market/research evidence, decision discipline and a governed learning architecture that evaluates past decisions before any model change can reach production.

## User problem

Retail investors often use disconnected tools:
- one app tracks holdings;
- another shows market prices;
- research is scattered across news and filings;
- AI tools can produce opinions without preserving what was known at the time;
- there is rarely a transparent process for measuring whether prior decisions were good or bad.

MINBEIS connects the complete decision lifecycle.

## What works today

- portfolio transactions, position value, cost and P/L;
- US and Euronext Athens support with fail-closed quote integrity;
- portfolio allocation/concentration and P/L contribution;
- local portfolio history;
- privacy ownership isolation and recovery of legacy local data;
- research dossiers and opportunity analysis;
- human-approved MINBEIS decision layer;
- immutable decision journal;
- 7/30/90-day outcome tracking;
- market-regime/event/reason context learning;
- append-only historical event archive;
- strict point-in-time replay;
- learning review queue;
- prospective shadow challengers;
- controlled promotion gates;
- no autonomous broker execution;
- no automatic production-policy mutation.

## Technical moat

### 1. Time-aware auditability
The system stores what it knew when a decision was made and protects that context with integrity hashing.

### 2. Learning without hindsight
Historical replay cannot use evidence or market data that appeared after the replay timestamp.

### 3. Prospective validation
A proposed model change is validated only on decisions that occur after the proposal was created.

### 4. Governed promotion
A challenger must satisfy sample, diversity and chronological-stability gates before it can even become a promotion candidate.

### 5. Fail-closed data integrity
Missing, stale or identity-mismatched data blocks the affected path rather than silently fabricating an answer.

## Current limitations

MINBEIS does not yet claim:
- statistically proven alpha;
- superior investment returns;
- causal event-price relationships;
- production-proven autonomous self-improvement.

Those require matured prospective OOS evidence.
