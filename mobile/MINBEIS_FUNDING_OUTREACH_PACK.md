# MINBEIS — Funding Outreach Pack

## Core opening

MINBEIS is a working Android investment decision-support product with a differentiated governed-learning architecture.

Unlike a conventional portfolio tracker, it records what the system knew at decision time, measures later outcomes, replays historical events without future-information leakage, proposes bounded model changes and validates them prospectively in shadow mode before any human-approved promotion.

We are looking for support to move from technically validated MVP to external commercial validation: licensed data, structured beta, production distribution, privacy/security/legal review and prospective model validation.

## 30-second version

MINBEIS combines portfolio tracking with an auditable AI decision lifecycle. It does not automatically trade and it does not silently retrain itself. Every learning proposal is frozen, tested only on future observations and blocked from production unless it passes predefined evidence gates and receives explicit approval.
