# MINBEIS — 12-Month Funding Budget / Use of Funds

Status: planning model for sponsor/investor conversations.
Important: these are internal planning assumptions, not supplier quotations.

## Funding objective

Convert the current technically validated MVP into a commercially testable, legally reviewed, production-distributed product with enough prospective usage/outcome evidence to evaluate the governed-learning system.

## Funding-stage discipline

### Prize / accelerator ask
Use the exact programme amount/award structure. Explain that proceeds fund the next validation milestones.

### Early sponsor / strategic partner
Target non-dilutive support, data credits, infrastructure credits, pilot access or distribution partnership where possible.

### Angel / pre-seed
Do not anchor the discussion solely to this budget. First establish:
- validated problem;
- beta engagement;
- evidence of willingness to pay;
- data economics;
- regulatory path;
- credible 12–18 month milestones.
